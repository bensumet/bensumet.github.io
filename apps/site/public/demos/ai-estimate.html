Round 6 · K1·A range visual (reads as a range, not a probability)

Feedback on S3 Bars was right: tall bars around the estimate imply "most likely here", and the model does not tell us that. It gives us three numbers: estimate, low and high. Every visual below draws only those three numbers on a straight value scale, with nothing implied between them. S2, S3 and S4 are removed from this switch for the same reason; S1 Pin stays as the baseline. S9 to S11 go beyond a bar, but the table shows they are easy to misread. S12 to S14 are built on the clearest range convention (capped ends, a brace, boundary posts). New: S6·A to S6·C are three polished versions of the dumbbell, the clearest of all. Each draws itself in from the estimate outward when the card opens (switch styles or reopen the card to replay it).

No new hues. The only additions are alpha (for glows) and mixing a palette colour into background.paper (for the dark wash). One gap: the dark theme has only one saturated violet, pastel.purple.light #5229BC. It is too dark for text on dark paper, so text gradients in dark mode run blue to light blue, and the violet is used only in fills and glows.
