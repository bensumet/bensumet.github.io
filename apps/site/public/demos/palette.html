The ten tones, side by side

Measured, not eyeballed: the readout under the strip gives the closest pair in the set by CIE-Lab distance across all 36 pairs. In light, Revision 1 bottoms out at ΔE 4.8 (Insights vs Renewal) and the proposal reaches 15.2. In dark the shipped set bottoms out at 9.5 (Insights vs Draft) and the proposal reaches 18.1. Switch between them in light mode and watch Insights. Revision 1 deepened success, error, warning and assistant to solid 100–200 fills but left info on an 8%-alpha brand tint and the three accents on 50-level pastels — so the set now reads as two families rather than one. Before the revision they were all pale, and consistent.

Shadow budget

You asked whether we use too much drop shadow. Counted across apps/ and packages/, the answer is the opposite — the app is about four-fifths flat already, and the risk is that shadows are rare enough to be applied inconsistently rather than that there are too many.

The rule that fits what the code already does: a shadow means “this floats above the page and can be dismissed”. Drawers, menus, popovers, dialogs and snackbars get one. Anything that lives in the page — cards, panels, accordions, the grid, and the search dropdown, which is mounted flush to the input — gets a border and a surface step instead. That keeps the drawer’s shadow meaningful, which is the one you said you liked.

Contrast audit

Every row is computed in the browser from the palette values themselves, compositing alpha over its real backdrop. AA = 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for non-text boundaries that identify a control (WCAG 1.4.11). Surface-separation rows have no legal minimum — they measure whether one plane is distinguishable from another at all.
